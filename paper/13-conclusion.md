# Conclusion

The project began from the intuition that Bitcoin, the one thing nobody can issue, backdate or speed
up, could be a healthy limit on AI. Checked at every level available, the intuition survives only in
a small form. Bitcoin can hold an ordered record of what an AI agent committed to, hard to backdate
or fork beyond a depth chosen for one's risk, and today's script can rate-limit an agent's releases,
let any guardian veto them and let its authority lapse in block height. Scored in rounds against a
baseline that meets H0, a record of forecasts costs a faker identities in proportion to its score,
once each bond backs one chain. Everything else broke or proved to be prior work: the record rewards
capability, not alignment, and its standing rewards bets; one guardian can end it and the guardians
together can write it; the lab still chooses its rewards; and a cheap fresh identity is still a
harmful agent's way back in. The design, its ledger and its regtest checks are public, so that
anyone who thinks a claim here is wrong, or too modest, can show it.
