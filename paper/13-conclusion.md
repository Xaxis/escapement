# Conclusion

The intuition this project started from was that Bitcoin, the one thing nobody can issue, backdate
or speed up, could be a healthy limit on AI. Tested at every level we could, the intuition survives
in a smaller and more specific form. Bitcoin can hold a complete record of what an AI agent
committed to, under an identity that costs something, and such a record has properties no registry
run by one party offers to rivals: it cannot be backdated, forked or quietly trimmed. Scored with
care, a record of forecasts is expensive to fake in proportion to what it claims, a training reward
drawn from it cannot be chosen after the fact, and the parties who gain from reading records alone
can make reading them the norm. Around the record, today's script can rate-limit an agent's
releases, let any guardian veto them, and let its authority lapse in block height without anyone's
clock.

What did not survive matters as much. A cheap fresh identity is still a harmful agent's way back
in, the scorers are trusted parties, a reward on outcomes the agent can move rewards manipulation,
and a veto is only as final as the blocks on top of it. These are the open problems, and section 12
names the experiments that would settle them. The design is public, its numbers reproduce from one
ledger, and its Bitcoin pieces from one command, so that anyone who thinks a claim here is wrong can
show it.
